{% trans %} The Digital Government Excellence Seals are a recognition from the Ministry of Information Technologies and Communications (MinTIC) for digital services and open datasets that meet high standards of quality, security, accessibility, and transparency. It certifies that the State's digital products respond to citizens' needs, comply with regulations, promote trust and participation, and guarantee continuous improvement. For open data, it ensures that it is useful, up-to-date, and reusable. {% endtrans %}
